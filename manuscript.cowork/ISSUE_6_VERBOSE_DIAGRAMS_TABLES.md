1. **"Architecture of Trust" diagram (7 occurrences)** - Wayfinding with "YOU ARE HERE" indicators

## Why This Differs from the Original Estimate

The original editor recommendation assumed repeated diagrams and tables were visual redundancy. Upon context analysis:

1. **Diagrams don't contribute to word count** - Mermaid code renders as images; the "words" in diagrams aren't counted in manuscript word totals.

2. **Each occurrence has unique state** - The "YOU ARE HERE" indicators, completion checkmarks, and "Actual" budget values change across chapters.

3. **Non-linear reading requires repetition** - Readers who jump to Chapter 6 need the context that Chapter 4 readers already have.

4. **Visual anchoring aids retention** - Educational research shows that repeated visual frameworks help readers build mental models.
